# Instrument development and content-validity plan — v0.2.0 candidate

## Scientific identity

ILP-EPL, ILP-MEA and ILP-IVP are original provisional instruments. They are not Kolb, Felder-Silverman or Kuder instruments, adaptations or validated equivalents. A shared renderer never implies common questions, options, scoring or interpretations.

## Reproducible item lifecycle

Each item must have an immutable identifier, instrument/version, construct/domain, rationale, source type, author, creation date, wording history, response format, scoring key version, accessibility notes, expert decisions, cognitive-interview findings and rights status.

## Content-validity study

The protocol requires qualified experts and members of the target population. Before recruitment, the research team must freeze expert eligibility, conflict-of-interest rules, rating dimensions, qualitative-comment process, decision thresholds, disagreement handling and revision rounds. Numerical indices cannot replace substantive expert arguments.

## Cognitive interviews

Iterative rounds must evaluate comprehension, retrieval, judgment, response mapping, accessibility, cultural relevance and unintended harm. Sampling must include relevant diversity. Every revision creates a new instrument version; no live response may be reinterpreted under silently changed wording.

## Rights and originality

No proprietary item may be copied merely because its wording is publicly visible. The item-level register must distinguish original wording, lawful quotation, public-domain material and authorized adaptation. Publication and software licenses apply only to material owned or lawfully controlled by the project.
