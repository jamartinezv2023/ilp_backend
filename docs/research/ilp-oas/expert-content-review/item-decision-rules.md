# Item decision rules

## Scale anchors

For relevance, clarity, comprehensibility, representativeness, accessibility and cultural appropriateness: 1 = not adequate; 2 = major revision required; 3 = adequate with minor revision; 4 = highly adequate. Essentiality: 1 = not necessary; 2 = useful but not essential; 3 = essential.

BiasRisk accepts only None, Minor or Major. Recommendation accepts only Retain, Revise or Remove.

## Quantitative summaries

- I-CVI is calculated separately for every criterion as the proportion of eligible independent reviewers assigning 3 or 4.
- S-CVI/Ave is the mean of item-level I-CVI values within one instrument and one criterion.
- Universal agreement may be reported only as a supplementary stringent statistic.
- Modified kappa may be reported with the exact number of reviewers and chance-agreement formula documented by the analysis script.
- Essentiality summaries and CVR, if used, remain separate from I-CVI.

No threshold is treated as universal proof of validity. Before opening the review round, the panel size, missing-rating rule and criterion-specific thresholds must be frozen in a dated analysis decision. Small-panel values are interpreted with their uncertainty and qualitative evidence.

## Disposition

Every item receives one recorded decision: retain unchanged, revise, split, merge, remove or defer. Major bias/accessibility concerns cannot be overridden by a favorable average without written rationale. Changes require a new immutable candidate version and re-review when meaning changes. Results from different instruments or different review criteria are never collapsed into one global validity score.
