# Expert content-review protocol - ILP-OAS v0.4.0 candidate

## Purpose

Evaluate candidate content evidence before cognitive interviews or field administration. Expert judgment addresses item relevance, clarity, adolescent comprehensibility, construct representativeness, accessibility, Colombian cultural and linguistic appropriateness, potential bias and proposed revision. It does not establish reliability, factor structure, predictive validity, measurement invariance or diagnostic utility.

## Panel composition

Recruitment is a later governed activity. The intended independent panel is 6 to 10 reviewers whose combined expertise covers psychometrics or educational measurement; secondary education and adolescent development; inclusive education and accessibility; Colombian Spanish and cultural context; and the constructs represented by the three instruments. One reviewer may cover more than one domain, but the final matrix must show coverage of every domain.

## Independence and conflicts

Every reviewer receives a pseudonymous code and completes eligibility, independence and conflict declarations before access to item forms. Financial, supervisory, authorship or close collaboration conflicts are recorded and dispositioned. Project authors may provide construct clarification but do not count as independent reviewers.

## Rating procedure

1. Review construct definitions before item wording.
2. Review each instrument independently, without cross-instrument scoring.
3. Rate each item on six 4-point criteria: relevance, clarity, comprehensibility, representativeness, accessibility and cultural appropriateness.
4. Rate essentiality on the three-category field provided.
5. Record bias risk, recommendation, rationale and an optional proposed revision.
6. Complete the global instrument evaluation only after all items in that instrument.
7. Do not communicate ratings to other reviewers before the independent round closes.

## Safeguards

No student data are collected. Expert recruitment and receipt of completed forms require institutional ethics or governance determination, information notice, data-minimization plan and secure transfer procedure. Empty templates may be inspected before those approvals. No item is released publicly or licensed until rights, authorship and originality review is completed.

## Version control

The reviewed specifications are immutable version 0.1.0-candidate. Every proposed edit creates a decision record and, if accepted, a new instrument version. Completed ratings are never overwritten.
