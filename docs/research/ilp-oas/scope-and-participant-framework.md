# Scope and participant framework — ILP-OAS v0.2.1 candidate

## Primary validation population

- Target population: Students enrolled in secondary and upper-secondary education within formally authorized Colombian educational institutions.
- Educational range: Grades 9 through 11.
- Expected age range: Approximately 14 to 18 years, while recognizing legitimate grade-age variation and avoiding arbitrary exclusion based only on age.
- Initial geographic and institutional scope: Formally authorized educational institutions in Colombia.
- Initial pilot context: One or more institutions in Valle del Cauca with an agreement or written institutional authorization and prior ethics approval.
- Administration language: Colombian Spanish, using clear, accessible and inclusive language.

The primary validation population is deliberately delimited. Findings cannot be generalized automatically beyond the approved sampling frame, institutional contexts, language, educational levels or observed participant characteristics.

## Future independent extension

Higher-education students constitute a separate future population. Their inclusion requires an independent protocol phase for adaptation, content review, cognitive interviewing, replication and measurement-invariance analysis. Higher-education responses must not be pooled with the primary grades 9–11 validation sample merely to increase sample size.

## Unit of analysis

The primary unit is one consented/assented participant completing one immutable version of an instrument administration. Repeated administrations, classrooms and institutions require explicit identifiers that permit longitudinal or multilevel analysis without exposing direct identity in analytical datasets.

## Candidate inclusion criteria

1. Enrolment in grades 9–11 at an institution included in the approved sampling frame.
2. Ability to access an approved administration mode with reasonable accommodations.
3. Valid guardian consent and participant assent when legally or ethically required, or participant consent where applicable.
4. Voluntary participation without academic penalty, inducement or coercion.

## Candidate exclusion and withdrawal rules

- Absence of the required consent/assent pathway.
- Duplicate, technically corrupted or demonstrably ineligible records under prespecified rules.
- Participant withdrawal, honored without penalty and with deletion handling defined in the approved data-management plan.
- Age alone is not an automatic exclusion when legitimate grade-age variation exists; exceptional cases must follow the approved protocol.

Missing items and partial responses are not automatic exclusions. Their treatment must be prespecified and reported transparently.

## Generalizability boundary

Initial evidence will support claims only for populations and contexts sufficiently represented by the approved design. Extension to other Colombian regions, countries, languages, educational stages or higher education requires new evidence for relevance, response processes, measurement properties and, when comparisons are intended, invariance and differential item functioning.
