# Psychometric validation and analysis plan — v0.2.0 candidate

## Design principle

Every measurement property receives its own research question, sample justification, design, analysis, uncertainty estimate and decision rule. COSMIN is used as an adapted methodological aid, not as a claim that ILP-OAS instruments are PROMs.

## Required staged studies

1. Content validity and cognitive interviewing.
2. Feasibility pilot and response-process analysis.
3. Dimensionality study with ordinal-appropriate EFA/CFA and competing models.
4. Internal consistency only after adequate dimensionality evidence.
5. Test-retest reliability and measurement error with a justified interval.
6. Item-level modelling (IRT/Rasch) only when response format, assumptions and sample support it.
7. Measurement invariance and DIF before substantive group-score comparisons.
8. Convergent, discriminant, criterion-related or predictive hypotheses frozen before analysis.
9. Cross-validation or independent replication before stable operational claims.

## Statistical safeguards

Sample sizes must be justified separately rather than by one universal ratio. Report uncertainty intervals, missing-data handling, multiplicity decisions, estimator assumptions, model convergence, sensitivity analyses and all deviations. Do not optimize cut-offs on the same data used to claim validation.

## Interpretation boundary

Scores describe contextual educational responses under a specific version and cannot diagnose a clinical condition, fix a learner into a permanent type, deny opportunity or trigger an autonomous high-impact decision.
