# Ethics and institutional review application summary

## Study

Working title: Expert content review of three original ILP-OAS educational instruments for Colombian grades 9-11.

## Purpose

Obtain structured evidence about relevance, comprehensiveness, comprehensibility, clarity, representativeness, accessibility, cultural appropriateness and possible bias before cognitive interviews or student administration.

## Participants in this phase

Target: eight independent adult professionals, recruited up to a maximum of ten candidates to achieve the planned eligible panel. Collective expertise must cover seven predefined domains. Project item authors are not counted as independent experts.

## Procedures

Eligibility and conflict review; approved participation information; confidential instrument access; independent 4-point item ratings and qualitative comments; global evaluation of each instrument; pseudonymized analysis; documented adjudication. Expected time and burden remain PENDING pilot estimation and external review.

## Data

Contact and qualification evidence, expertise domains, conflict declarations, participation evidence, item ratings, comments and timestamps. Identities are separated from rating data. No students, health data, biometrics or automated high-impact decisions are involved in this phase.

## Risks and protections

Potential professional discomfort, confidentiality breach, re-identification, unintended disclosure of candidate items and perceived pressure. Controls include voluntary participation, no adverse consequence for declining, independent ratings, conflict disposition, data minimization, pseudonymous codes, approved secure storage, access control and limited retention.

## Status

Candidate protocol version 0.6.0; external submission, ethics approval, exemption, risk classification, data-controller designation and recruitment authorization are not yet recorded.
