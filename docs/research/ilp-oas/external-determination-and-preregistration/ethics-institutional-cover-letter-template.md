# Candidate cover letter for institutional or ethics determination

STATUS: DRAFT - NOT SUBMITTED

To: Competent institutional authority or research-ethics body

Subject: Request for written determination - ILP-OAS original educational instrument development

The researcher requests a written determination of the applicable institutional and ethics pathway for an initial study involving independent adult professional experts who will review 84 candidate educational-instrument items. This phase collects no student data and performs no intervention. It may collect reviewer qualifications, conflicts, participation evidence, item ratings and qualitative comments.

Please determine, without relying on the researcher's self-classification: oversight responsibility; applicability of ethics review; risk category; information and consent or authorization requirements; data-protection requirements; confidentiality conditions for candidate items; permitted recruitment and transfer procedures; amendments; reporting obligations; and validity period of the determination.

No expert will be contacted and no rating will be collected until the applicable determinations and a separate operational authorization are documented.
