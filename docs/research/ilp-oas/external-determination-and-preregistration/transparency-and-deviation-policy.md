# Transparency and deviation policy

The preregistered plan distinguishes confirmatory decision rules from exploratory observations. Any change after viewing real ratings is labeled, dated, justified and reported as a deviation or amendment. Original submissions, frozen files and hashes are retained under the approved schedule.

No threshold crossing alone is described as instrument validation. Content-review evidence is reported with panel composition, item-level denominators, missingness, qualitative concerns and uncertainty. Later cognitive interviewing, accessibility evaluation and psychometric studies remain separate.

No identifiable reviewer data, confidential completed forms or candidate item banks are made public through preregistration. Sharing decisions follow institutional approval, rights review and data-protection obligations.
