# Preregistration draft - ILP-OAS expert content review

STATUS: COMPLETE DRAFT, NOT SUBMITTED OR TIMESTAMPED

## Research questions

1. To what extent do independent experts judge each candidate item relevant to its assigned construct and dimension?
2. To what extent are the item wording, instructions, response scales and recall periods comprehensible and clear for the intended Colombian grades 9-11 context?
3. Do the item sets adequately represent the intended content domains, including accessibility and cultural appropriateness?
4. Which items require retention, revision, splitting, merging, removal or deferral before cognitive interviews?

## Design

Structured independent expert content review of three original instruments. No student participation and no psychometric field validation occur in this study. Eight eligible independent reviewers are planned; up to ten candidates may be contacted only after authorization. Every item requires a target of eight and a minimum of six valid ratings.

## Outcomes

Six separate 4-point criteria per item: relevance, clarity, comprehensibility, representativeness, accessibility and cultural appropriateness. Essentiality, bias risk, recommendation, proposed revision and rationale remain separate fields.

## Analysis

For every item and criterion, I-CVI equals the proportion of eligible observed ratings equal to 3 or 4. S-CVI/Ave is calculated separately by instrument and criterion. Universal agreement and modified kappa are supplementary. The decision value for I-CVI is 0.78 and the instrument-level S-CVI/Ave target is 0.90. These values guide adjudication and do not automatically prove validity. No missing rating is imputed. Fewer than six valid ratings yields insufficient review and requires additional independent review.

## Exclusions

Reviewer data may be excluded only for a preregistered governance reason: prior ineligibility, unmitigated conflict, duplicate submission, confirmed coordination or copying, or invalid/incomplete protocol. Ratings are never excluded because they are unfavorable.

## Qualitative integration

All major bias or accessibility concerns receive written disposition even when numerical values meet targets. Revisions that change meaning create a new immutable candidate version and may require another expert round.

## Transparency

All deviations, missingness, exclusions, instrument versions and unfavorable results will be reported. Instruments and criteria will not be collapsed into one global validity score. The draft may be submitted to a zero-cost OSF Registration only after external and institutional review; public versus embargoed status will be selected without uploading identifiable or confidential data.
