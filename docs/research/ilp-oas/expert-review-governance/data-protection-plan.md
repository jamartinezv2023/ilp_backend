# Data-protection plan for expert review

## Data map

The study may process reviewer contact information, qualifications, expertise evidence, conflict declarations, eligibility decisions, consent or authorization evidence, item ratings, comments and timestamps. No student data are collected in expert review.

## Roles to determine externally

The responsible data controller, any processor, institutional owner, privacy contact and rights-request channel are PENDING and must be named before recruitment. The researcher does not substitute a personal email account or public repository for an approved research-data location.

## Minimization and separation

Use a random reviewer code in rating data. Store names and contact details only in a separate encrypted linkage file when operationally necessary. Do not place names, emails, CVs, signatures, completed conflict forms or completed ratings in Git, public cloud folders or evidence transcripts.

## Purpose and access

Use data only to establish reviewer eligibility, manage the review, analyze content evidence and document defensible instrument decisions. Grant least-privilege access. Log exports and transfers. Prohibit reuse for employment, performance evaluation, marketing or automated profiling.

## Retention and rights

Retention periods, deletion method, archival exceptions, withdrawal effects and the channel for access, correction, proof of authorization, information about use, complaint and deletion requests remain PENDING institutional approval. The approved schedule supersedes this candidate plan.

## Incident response

Stop collection, preserve logs, contain access, assess affected data, notify the institutional responsible party and follow the approved breach procedure. Do not conceal or independently improvise a notification decision.
