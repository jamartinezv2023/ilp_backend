# Reviewer selection and exclusion

## Intended panel

The target is 6 to 10 eligible independent reviewers. Final size is frozen before invitations and must remain methodologically defensible if nonresponse reduces the panel.

## Inclusion criteria

- Verifiable postgraduate formation or equivalent documented expertise relevant to at least one required domain.
- Normally at least three years of relevant professional, research or instrument-development experience; justified exceptions require a written decision.
- Ability to review Spanish-language items for Colombian grades 9-11.
- Acceptance of confidentiality, data-processing information, independent review and conflict disclosure.
- Availability to complete one instrument at a time and provide qualitative rationale.

## Required collective coverage

The final panel must collectively cover: psychometrics or educational measurement; Colombian secondary education and adolescent development; inclusive education and accessibility; Colombian Spanish and cultural context; ILP-EPL construct content; ILP-MEA construct content; and ILP-IVP construct content.

## Exclusion or non-independent classification

- Direct author of the candidate item wording.
- Undisclosed material financial, supervisory, familial or close collaborative conflict.
- Inability to complete the independence, confidentiality or data-governance requirements.
- Submission containing coordinated or copied ratings.
- Missing qualifications that leave no documented basis for the assigned expertise domain.

Project authors may clarify constructs and adjudicate decisions but their judgments are not counted as independent expert ratings. Eligibility decisions are recorded before forms are issued and are never inferred after seeing a favorable or unfavorable score.
