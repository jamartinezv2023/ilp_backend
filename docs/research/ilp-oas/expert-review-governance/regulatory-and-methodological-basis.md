# Regulatory and methodological basis - ILP-OAS expert review

## Scope

This register records the official sources considered while preparing governance. It does not provide legal advice, classify the study risk or replace a decision by the competent institution or research-ethics body.

## Colombian research governance

- Colombia Ministry of Health, Resolution 8430 of 1993: scientific, technical and administrative standards for research involving human beings in health contexts. Official text: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/sites/rid/Lists/BibliotecaDigital/RIDE/de/dij/resolucion-8430-DE-1993.PDF
- The ILP-OAS study is educational research. Applicability, risk category, consent requirements and review pathway must therefore be determined in writing by the competent institution or ethics body; this project does not self-declare exemption or approval.

## Personal data

- Colombian Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 establishes general personal-data protection requirements and principles including legality, purpose, freedom, truthfulness, transparency, restricted access, security and confidentiality. Official text: https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=49981
- Regulatory compilation for Decree 1377 of 2013: https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=53646
- Reviewer identity, contact details, qualifications, conflicts and responses are treated as personal data when linked or linkable to a person. Data minimization, prior information, authorization or other documented lawful basis, access control and retention disposition are required before collection.

## Content-validity methodology

- COSMIN study-design checklist: https://www.cosmin.nl/wp-content/uploads/COSMIN-study-designing-checklist_final.pdf
- COSMIN resources: https://www.cosmin.nl/

COSMIN informs relevance, comprehensiveness and comprehensibility review, but ILP-OAS is an educational instrument family rather than a clinical outcome measure. COSMIN is used as a methodological reference, not as an automatic certification framework.

## Version note

The legal and institutional basis must be rechecked immediately before recruitment because standards, institutional policies and national rules may change. The dated external determination controls if it imposes stricter requirements.
