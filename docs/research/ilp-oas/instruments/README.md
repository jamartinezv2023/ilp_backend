# ILP original instrument family - candidate specifications

This directory contains complete candidate item banks for three distinct instruments:

- ILP-EPL: 24 items, 4 dimensions, contextual experiential-learning processes.
- ILP-MEA: 30 items, 5 dimensions, strategies and supports for learning.
- ILP-IVP: 30 items, 6 dimensions, interest in families of activities.

They share a renderer contract but do not share constructs, instructions, response scales, scoring interpretations or item banks. They are original project drafts, not adaptations or validated equivalents of Kolb, Felder-Silverman, Kuder or any other proprietary instrument.

Status: candidate, unvalidated, not authorized for field collection. Item wording was drafted with AI assistance and requires documented human authorship, expert content review, cognitive interviews, accessibility review and rights determination before licensing or use with participants.
