# Population, territory and institutional access statement

## Primary scope

The primary study is intended for students enrolled in grades 9-11 at officially authorized educational institutions within the Certified Territorial Entity in Education of Palmira, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. The expected age range is approximately 14-18 years, while legitimate variation between age and grade is permitted.

## Sampling and site boundary

The expression "any institution in Palmira" defines the eligible sampling frame; it does not authorize unrestricted access and does not mean that every institution will participate. Each site requires, at minimum:

1. documented institutional invitation or permission;
2. the applicable research-ethics determination;
3. data-protection and custody approval;
4. parental or guardian consent and student assent when legally and ethically required;
5. a version-frozen instrument and approved administration protocol;
6. a site-specific implementation and incident-response plan.

Results will not be generalized beyond the sampled population, institutions, grades, period and conditions without explicit analysis and justification.
