# Methodological evolution and alignment memorandum

## Historical document

The research progress report dated 27 May 2025 is retained as historical evidence of the doctoral trajectory. It described a preliminary platform organized around Kolb, Felder-Silverman and Kuder instruments and used diagnostic and classification language.

## Current protocol

The current ILP-OAS candidate protocol supersedes that preliminary instrument specification for prospective research activities. It defines three original candidate instruments:

- ILP-EPL: 24 items;
- ILP-MEA: 30 items;
- ILP-IVP: 30 items.

These 84 candidate items are distinct instruments with their own construct definitions, dimensions, response structures, scoring specifications and interpretation limits, although they may share a generic software renderer.

## Scientific boundary

- No equivalence with Kolb, Felder-Silverman or Kuder is claimed.
- No diagnostic, clinical, deterministic or high-stakes use is permitted.
- No global score or categorical band is enabled at the candidate stage.
- Content review, cognitive interviews, rights and originality audit, pilot testing and psychometric validation remain pending.
- The software renderer is not evidence of validity.
- Prior favorable doctoral progress evaluations evidence academic progression; they do not constitute research-ethics approval, instrument validation or site authorization.

This memorandum prevents historical descriptions from being represented as the current prospective methodology while preserving an auditable account of protocol evolution.
