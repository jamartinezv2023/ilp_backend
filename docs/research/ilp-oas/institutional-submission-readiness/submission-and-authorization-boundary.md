# Submission and authorization boundary

## Permitted after this cycle

- Review this package with the doctoral director or designated academic authority.
- Identify the official University of Alicante and Palmira submission routes.
- Prepare institution-specific cover letters without naming an institution as participating.
- Obtain documentary determinations and file them in the controlled external-evidence intake area.

## Not permitted by this cycle

- Representing the package as submitted, approved or preregistered.
- Contacting minors or collecting student, family, teacher or expert data.
- Recruiting experts or collecting expert ratings.
- Deploying the candidate instruments in production.
- Activating scores, bands, diagnoses or automated educational decisions.
- Executing A9 until authentic external evidence has been received and verified.

Personal identifiers, government identifiers and personal email addresses must remain outside Git and public audit transcripts. Only the minimum necessary information should be included in the actual private submission made through an approved channel.
