# Institutional submission readiness - ILP-OAS

Status: **PREPARATION ONLY - NOT SUBMITTED**

This package prepares the scientific and documentary basis for institutional review of the ILP-OAS research programme. It does not record ethics approval, institutional authorization, preregistration, expert recruitment, field implementation or data collection.

## Institutional frame

- Doctoral programme: Doctorate in Educational Technology Applied to Knowledge (TEAC), University of Alicante.
- Research line: Research and diagnostic methods applied to diversity, equality and educational inclusion in the knowledge society.
- Initial Colombian setting: officially authorized educational institutions within the Certified Territorial Entity in Education of Palmira, Valle del Cauca.
- Primary population: students enrolled in grades 9-11, expected age approximately 14-18 years with legitimate age-grade variation.
- Higher education: excluded from the primary study and reserved for a future independent adaptation, replication and measurement-invariance study.

The named institution or institutions will be selected only after documented institutional access, ethics determination and authorization. No institution is presumed to participate.
